Questions

Who builds the portfolio?

CodPox developers design and develop it for you.

Which pack should I pick?

Minimal Design if you want a clean portfolio online quickly. Pro Design if you have several projects to show. Design Pro if you want a high-level custom look. Not sure? Ask us on WhatsApp after you send the form.

What will my address look like?

A subdomain in your name, such as yourname.codpox.com. If the name you want is taken, we will agree on another with you.

How many redesigns do I get?

Minimal Design includes 2, Pro Design includes 5 and Design Pro includes 8, all within the length of your pack. Tell us what you want to change and we will confirm it with you on WhatsApp.

What happens when my pack ends?

Your address stays live for the full length of your pack. We will message you before it ends so we can agree on the next step together.

How do I pay?

After you send the form, we contact you on WhatsApp, confirm your pack and share the payment details there.

Do I need to be in CodPox or SXC?

Your portfolio shows the work you have built. Send your request and we will confirm the details with you.
